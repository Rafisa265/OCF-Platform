Our Story

From a small workshop in Arba Minch to customers in 30+ countries — preserving Oromo culture one garment at a time.

How It All Started

OCF was founded in 2019 by a team of Oromo cultural enthusiasts and fashion designers who saw a gap: the diaspora community desperately wanted authentic traditional clothing but had no reliable source.

What began as a small workshop connecting 12 artisans to diaspora customers has grown into a platform serving 200+ artisan partners across five Oromo regions, with customers in 30+ countries.

We believe that fashion is one of the most powerful vehicles of cultural identity. Every OCF garment is not just clothing — it's a statement, a memory, and a connection to heritage.
